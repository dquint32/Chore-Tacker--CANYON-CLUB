How to use this app

Instructions

Check off a chore the moment you finish it — on the Home tab. No login needed.

  1. Tap the chore you just finished (find it under Opening, Mid-Day, Closing, or Sometimes).
  2. Type your name. The next step unlocks once you do.
  3. Check the box: “I confirm I completed this chore 100%.” This unlocks the signature.
  4. Sign with your finger in the white box (use Clear to redo).
  5. Tap Submit. The chore turns amber and shows your name on every phone.

Made a mistake? Tap the amber chore again and choose Undo — it reopens so it can be redone. Green (approved) chores are locked; ask a lead.

Already checked off? If a chore is greyed out with a name, it's done — don't redo it.

Review and approve finished chores on the Sign-Off tab.

  1. Tap the Sign-Off tab. The badge shows how many chores are waiting.
  2. Enter the lead PIN on the keypad. (Leads only — keep it off the guard floor.)
  3. Type your name once in “Lead's Name” — it's recorded on each approval.
  4. Check each card: chore, who did it, the time, and their signature.
  5. Approve (turns it green & locked) or Reject (removes it and reopens the chore).

How saving works: the app quietly connects to the database on open — no accounts, no passwords for guards. Completions sync to every phone in about a second, and the list resets fresh each day.
